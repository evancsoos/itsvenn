# Research: Arkansas Dept. of Health Food Establishment Requirements

Primary source: AR State Board of Health "Rules Pertaining to Retail Food Establishments," eff. Oct 24, 2022 (healthy.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/Retail_Food_Establishment_Rule_.pdf — full text read; section citations are from it). ADH FAQ: healthy.arkansas.gov/.../food-protection-faq/.

## Permit + plan review (new establishment)
- **Plan review is mandatory BEFORE construction or conversion of an existing structure** (§ 8-201.10, Pf). Required submittals (§ 8-201.11): menu, anticipated volume, layout, mechanical schematics, finish schedules, equipment specs, SOPs. Submitted via ADH e-plan portal.
- **Plan review fee: 1% of total estimated project cost, min $50, max $500** (ADH FAQ).
- **Permit (Form EHP-99, rev. 9/2025): $35.00 per category** (Restaurant $35), submitted with fees **30 days before opening**; pre-opening inspection required; permits not transferable; $35 billed annually thereafter (healthy.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/Food-Permit-Application-10.2025.pdf).
- Separate ADH plumbing plan review: Protective Health Codes, 501-661-2678.
- **Local contact: Benton County Health Unit, 1200 W. Walnut St., Suite 2200, Rogers, AR 72756, (479) 986-1300** (M–F 8:00–4:30). Central Food Protection: 501-661-2171.
- No published plan-review turnaround — ask the Benton County EHS.

## Food Code edition + physical requirements that drive buildout
- Rule is FDA Food Code-based; FDA tracking lists Arkansas under the **2013 Food Code** as amended by the Oct 2022 AR rule (food-safety.com 2024 article; FDA live page returned 401 — flagged).
- **3-compartment sink** required, sized to largest equipment, with drainboards (§§ 4-301.11, 4-301.12).
- **Handwash sinks**: ≥1 plus as needed; in food prep, dispensing, and warewashing areas AND in/adjacent to toilet rooms (§§ 5-203.10, 5-204.10); ≥100°F water (§ 5-202.11); hand sinks single-purpose (§ 5-205.10). Practical: one behind bar + restroom.
- **Mop/service sink**: ≥1 service sink or curbed facility w/ floor drain (§ 5-203.12).
- **Hot water**: sized to peak demand, justified at plan review (§ 5-103.10(B)).
- **Cold holding 41°F or less** (Ch. 3).
- **Finishes**: smooth, durable, easily cleanable; nonabsorbent in moist areas (§§ 6-101.10, 6-201.10).
- **Hoods**: food rule only requires sufficiency (§ 4-301.13); Type I vs II is the **2021 Arkansas Mechanical Code (IMC 2021, eff. 7/1/2022)** — IMC § 507.2: Type I over grease/smoke appliances (fryers, griddles, ranges); Type II over heat/steam. Espresso machines and light-duty electric equipment generally avoid Type I; most commercial convection ovens DO require Type I; UL-certified ventless rapid-cook ovens avoid hoods. Appliance-by-appliance call by Bentonville mechanical inspector at plan review.
- **Grease interceptor**: AR Plumbing Code 2018 Ch. 10 requires grease control for grease-laden fixtures. **Bentonville runs a FOG Control Program (Pretreatment Ordinance 2019-185)** via Bentonville Water Utilities — pretreatment@bentonvillear.com, (479) 696-0293, portal fogregister.com/bentonville. A no-fry pastry/espresso café may qualify for a small under-sink trap or waiver — **get a written determination before committing**.

## Menu-driven tiers
- Inspection frequency by risk: 1×/yr (non-hazardous/prepackaged), 2×/yr (cook-and-serve), 3×/yr (cooling/reheating) (ADH FAQ). § 8-401.10(B)(iii) explicitly allows reduced frequency for coffee service + non-TCS prepackaged food.
- Rule defines a **"Coffee Kiosk"** category (coffee beverages + prepackaged non-refrigerated items only). A sit-down café with milk drinks will be a Restaurant ($35 — fee doesn't vary by risk), but a limited menu still buys: lower inspection frequency, simpler plan review, no Type I hood, minimal grease trap.
- Milk-based espresso drinks are TCS food → low-to-medium risk, not lowest tier.

## Staff certification
- **Certified Food Protection Manager required** (§ 2-102.11(A)) — ANSI/CFP-accredited (ServSafe, ~$150). **New establishments get 12 months from permit issuance to comply.** Exemption (§ 2-102.11(B)) for minimal-risk menus unlikely to apply if steaming milk.
- **No statewide food handler card requirement in Arkansas** — only the CFPM is mandated.

## Commissary/shared kitchen path
- Operating from someone else's approved kitchen allowed with permission letter + own permit (ADH FAQ). EHP-99 has an "Incubator/Contract Operator" checkbox. Viable pilot path (e.g., pastries baked in a licensed shared kitchen).

## Residential kitchens & second floor
- **Residential kitchens categorically barred**: § 6-202.20 — "A private home, a room used as living or sleeping quarters, or an area directly opening into a room used as living or sleeping quarters may not be used for conducting RETAIL FOOD ESTABLISHMENT operations. (P)" Also § 3-201.10(B). → The loft must be fully converted; no live/work hybrid.
- **No ADH rule against second-floor locations** — constraints are municipal (building/fire/ADA/duct routing/interceptor plumbing).

## Mocktails & under-21 service (the concept's legal foundation)
- **No ABC permit needed for zero-proof drinks**: Arkansas alcohol law applies to beverages **>0.5% ABV** (Ark. Code Title 3 definitions; ABC Rulebook, dfa.arkansas.gov).
- **NA beer/wine (<0.5%) to under-21**: Ark. Code § 3-3-202 prohibits furnishing to under-21 "any alcoholic beverage or a confectionary containing between 0.5% and 5% ABV" — beverages **below 0.5% fall outside the definition**, so Arkansas law does not prohibit selling NA beer to under-21 customers. NIAAA APIS lists Arkansas among states with no NA-beer restriction for minors (alcoholpolicy.niaaa.nih.gov AR profile).
- ⚠ Flag: this is interpretation from statutory definitions, not an explicit statute/AG opinion. For a concept deliberately serving 18–21: **get written confirmation from Arkansas ABC (DFA)**; note many NA brands self-impose 21+ policies.
