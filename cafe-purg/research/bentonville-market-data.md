# Research: Bentonville Local Market Data (2025–2026)

All claims sourced; confidence noted. Collected June 2026.

## Commercial retail lease rates (quantifies the family-space advantage)
- **UA Skyline Report 1H 2025 (authoritative local source, extracted from PDF, HIGH):** Bentonville retail Class A $25.38–$27.04/SF/yr; Class B $18.70–$20.13; Class C $11.52–$12.80; overall $18.50–$20.06. Retail vacancy only 3.4%. (walton.uark.edu/initiatives/cber/skyline-report/1H2025COMMReport.pdf, p.57–65)
- Downtown core spot listings: $30–$36/SF (208 SW 6th St $36; 406 SE 5th St $32) vs ~$17.50–$23 strip centers (commercialcafe.com, MEDIUM, June 2026 snapshot).
- LoopNet aggregates: ~$27/SF retail, ~$30/SF restaurant (MEDIUM; LoopNet/Crexi blocked direct fetch).
- NWA context: commercial vacancy 7.2% 1H2025; Bentonville $72.6M new commercial permits 1H2025, +835% HoH (talkbusiness.net, HIGH).
- **Implication: 1,000 sq ft on/near the square ≈ $30K–$36K/yr market rent. A family lease below market is a quantifiable five-figure annual subsidy.**

## Growth & tourism
- Bentonville population ~61,800 (2024), fastest-growing AR city 2020–24 (+14%) (talkbusiness.net/census, HIGH).
- Crystal Bridges: >800,000 visitors 2025; 100,000 SF expansion opened June 2026 (theartnewspaper.com, HIGH).
- Walmart new Home Office: opened Jan 2025, capacity 15,000+ employees (corporate.walmart.com, HIGH).
- Event tourism: $41M economic impact from 310 events in 2024 (2× prior record) (talkbusiness.net, HIGH).
- Cycling: 90,000+ bike tourists/yr; NWA trails $159M impact 2022 (oztrails.com, MEDIUM).

## Coffee scene & the gap
- Onyx expanding to 7 cafés 2025 (incl. 2 on Walmart campus); Airship opening 5th location (talkbusiness.net/sprudge.com, HIGH). Others: Kennedy, Heroes, Third Space, 211 Cafe, Doomsday.
- Onyx price points: latte $5.00, mocha $6.00, drip ~$2.50 (restaurantguru.com, LOW-MED crowd-sourced).
- **No dedicated late-night non-alcoholic venue found in NWA** — late-night guides list only bars/alcohol restaurants; coffee shops close late afternoon/early evening. ⚠ Flag: whitespace evidence, NOT measured demand — no survey data exists (nwatravelguide.com, LOW).

## City permitting (cross-checks earlier findings)
- Building permit fee schedule confirmed: $150K buildout ≈ $730 permit fee; flat $200 in-fill ≤$100K (bentonville.ar.gov/156, HIGH).
- Third-party estimate of TOTAL city fees for commercial TI incl. plan review, fire review, 3rd-party inspection: $2,000–$15,000 (permitplace.com, MEDIUM).
- Downtown Core (DC)/Downtown Edge zoning since 2009; Downtown Center Overlay around the square: brick/stone/plaster facades, 12-ft min ground-floor, glass storefronts; new unified development code in progress (Plan Bentonville Phase 2, 2025) (bentonvillebulletin.com / bentonvillear.com/880, MEDIUM — verify at bentonville.municipalcodeonline.com).

## ADH food permit (cross-checks earlier findings)
- Plan review: 1% of total estimated project cost, min $50 max $500, via ADH e-plan portal; submittals = floor/wall/ceiling schedules, menu, SOPs, equipment schedules (healthy.arkansas.gov FAQ + portal, HIGH).
- Annual retail food permit $35/yr (ADH application PDF 10.2025, HIGH).
- ADH (state) handles food permits — not Benton County.

## Labor
- Barista postings Bentonville: $12–$16/hr; example $11 base + tips = $16–$18 effective (ziprecruiter, MEDIUM/LOW).
- BLS food prep & serving mean, Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers MSA May 2024: $15.03/hr (bls.gov, MEDIUM — from search summary).

## Construction cost context
- RSMeans 2021 location factors: Arkansas ≈ 81–82% of national construction cost (rsmeans.com PDF, HIGH for 2021, MEDIUM applied to 2025–26).
- Arkansas residential benchmark ~$156/SF, among lowest states (home-cost.com, MEDIUM).
