# Research: Soft Costs, FF&E, Permits/Fees, Insurance, Monthly Opex

Small café (~1,000 sq ft, 25–40 seats), Arkansas, 2025–2026. Confidence noted per figure.

## Furniture & fixtures
- Commercial café seating: $60–$70/seat casual tier; $130–$140/seat solid wood (tableschairsbarstools.com, high). Per chair: $90–$150 basic / $150–$300 mid / $300–$600 upholstered (beaufurn.com, high).
- Table tops: $75–$150 laminate, $130–$250+ wood (voguedecor.com, medium). Booths $1,000–$3,000 each (beaufurn.com, medium).
- Contract lounge chairs ~$575–$2,100; sofas ~$1,300–$4,225 (affordableseating.net / contractfurniturestore.com, medium).
- Total furniture budget small café: $8,000–$30,000; modest design $8K–$15K (baristalife.co, high).
- Derived for 25–40 seats: $3,500–$14,000 dining + $3,000–$8,000 lounge pieces (medium).

## Counter/bar build
- Commercial millwork: $150–$300/LF basic, $300–$600 mid, $600–$1,200+ high-end (gwmillwork.com, high). 12–20 ft counter ≈ $1,800–$12,000 basic–mid (derived).
- Complete counter/service area incl. millwork: $10,000–$40,000 (baristalife.co, high).

## Lighting & decor
- Lighting + electrical $5,000–$25,000; paint/wall/decor $2,000–$10,000 (baristalife.co, high).
- Design fees ~8–20% of fit-out; designers $75–$150/hr (baristalife.co, medium).
- Full interior buildout context: $50–$200/sq ft → $60K–$150K for 800–1,200 sq ft (baristalife.co, medium).

## Architect/engineer
- Commercial architect 3–12% of construction cost (homeguide.com, high); TI per-SF method $1.50–$6.00/sq ft → ~$1,500–$6,000 for 1,000 sq ft (evstudio.com, high).
- Arkansas: stamped drawings required when occupancy classification/use changes; RDP seal mandatory for Group A/E/I, 3+ stories, or ≥5,000 sq ft. 1,000 sq ft café (<50 occupants → Group B) is a gray zone; most AR cities require stamped drawings for change of use — confirm with Bentonville building dept (medium, jurisdiction-dependent).

## Permits & licenses (Bentonville / Arkansas)
- Bentonville building permit fee schedule: $30 first $1,000 + $5/additional $1,000 up to $50K; $330 + $4/$1,000 from $50K–$100K → $75K buildout ≈ $430; $50K ≈ $275. Commercial tenant in-fill (never-occupied) flat $200 up to $100K. Fees DOUBLE if work starts before permit. (bentonville.ar.gov/156/Building-Permit-Fees, high)
- Plan review fee (commercial) $50; re-inspection $50 (permitplace.com, medium).
- ADH food service permit: $35/year + first-year plan review fee of 1% of estimated cost (min $50, max $500) (healthy.arkansas.gov FAQ; AR Code §20-57-204, high).
- City business license via Bentonville City Clerk; AR local licenses typically $50–$1,000 (tailorbrands.com / uaex.uada.edu brochure, medium).

## Insurance (annual)
- GL coffee shops ~$752/yr; BOP ~$1,105/yr ($1M/$2M) (insureon.com, high).
- Workers comp 3–5 employees ~$745/yr; food/bev avg ~$106/mo; restaurant benchmark ~$1.06 per $100 payroll, typically $1,000–$2,000/yr (insureon/kickstandinsurance/thehartford, high/medium).
- Package estimate: ~$2,000–$3,500/yr BOP + WC for 2–4 employees (derived, medium).

## Initial inventory
- Opening inventory $3,000–$8,000 (beans $800–$2,000; dairy/alt $400–$800; syrups $300–$600; disposables $400–$800) (baristalife.co, high). Corroborating $5,000–$10,000 (finmodelbuilder.com, medium).

## Branding & signage
- Logo $250–$1,000 typical (looka.com/fiverr, high); branding package $500–$10,000 (trttech.ca, medium).
- Channel letter sign $4,000–$20,000 (basic front-lit LED $2,000–$4,000) (flexlume.com, high); install $2,000–$5,000; sign permitting $300–$2,500 (signtag.com, medium); vinyl window graphics $10–$100 (homeadvisor, medium); blade sign typically $500–$3,000 (low).

## Monthly opex (2–4 staff, $0 rent family-owned)
- AR minimum wage $11.00/hr (labor.arkansas.gov, high). NWA barista $11–$13 base + tips (effective $15–$18) (ziprecruiter/indeed, high).
- Labor: 2–4 staff ≈ $4,000–$9,500/mo incl. ~10% payroll taxes (derived, medium).
- Utilities <4,000 sq ft: $500–$1,500/mo (baristalife.co/toasttab, high).
- COGS 20–30% of sales; prime cost incl. labor 50–55% (texascoffeeschool.com, high).
- POS: Square free plan, 2.6% + 15¢ in-person; Plus $49/mo (squareup.com, high).
- Music: Soundtrack Your Brand $29/mo/zone; direct PRO licensing $1,000–$1,500+/yr total (soundtrackyourbrand.com/musicforbusinessfinder.com, high).
- Internet $50–$150/mo; trash avg $178/mo; pest control $50–$150/mo (various, high/medium).
- **Derived total opex ex-COGS, $0 rent: ~$5,100–$11,700/mo. Working capital 3–6 months: ~$15K–$70K; practical target $25K–$50K** (derived, medium).

## Key takeaways
1. Bentonville permit fees are cheap for small TI: ~$275–$430 for a $50–$100K buildout (or flat $200 in-fill).
2. ADH health permit trivially cheap ($35/yr + ≤$500 plan review). Real soft-cost drivers: signage ($3K–$10K realistic), architect ($1.5K–$6K), furniture ($8K–$22K with lounge pieces).
3. With $0 rent, opex floor ~$5K–$12K/mo, dominated by labor; $25K–$50K working capital covers 3–6 months.
