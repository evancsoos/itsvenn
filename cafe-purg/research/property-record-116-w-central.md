## Millage / property tax
- DFA 2024 Millage Report (2025 collections), Benton County page: **City of Bentonville / Bentonville School District total = 61.82 mills** (school 46.8 + city 5.32 + county 9.7). Source: dfa.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024-millage-2025-collections.pdf (extracted from PDF p.8).
- Estimated annual property tax: $191,885 × 0.06182 ≈ **$11,863/yr**.
- Rule of thumb for buildout: every +$100K of improvement value adds 20% × 61.82 mills ≈ **+$1,236/yr** in property tax.
- Commercial property: no homestead credit or Amendment 79 homestead cap.
