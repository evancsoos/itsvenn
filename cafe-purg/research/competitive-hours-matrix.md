## The answer: does the square go dark for under-21s after ~9pm?
- **Weeknights (Mon–Thu): YES.** Every non-alcohol venue is closed by 9 (Onyx 8pm, Spark 9pm, library 8pm). After 9pm on a Tuesday, the only lit doors on the square serve alcohol as the primary product.
- **Weekends: mostly yes.** The single all-ages, non-alcohol venue on the square past 9pm is the Spark Café soda fountain (to 10). **A 17-year-old's realistic options at 9:30pm Saturday: one ice cream counter.**
- Even First Friday — the marquee all-ages event — ends at 9pm.

## Business-plan takeaways
1. A café open to 11pm–midnight would have **zero direct all-ages competitors at that hour** — weeknights especially.
2. **Pricing anchor:** downtown lattes $5.00–6.00, signature drinks $6.50–7.00 (Onyx matcha verified). Café Purg at $5.50–6.50 reads market-normal.
3. **Onyx proved the evening daypart:** only shop open to 8pm daily, at the highest prices in town — evening specialty coffee demand exists.
4. Differentiate vs Spark on seating/wifi/programming; borrow Meteor's all-ages-events model to own post-9pm.
5. **Watch 100 NW 2nd St (ex-Pressroom)** — prime corner, successor unannounced: both a threat (could fill the night gap first) and a market comp.
