# Research: Construction & Conversion Costs (Residential Loft → Café, 2nd Floor, NWA)

~800–1,200 sq ft, Bentonville AR context, 2025–2026. All claims sourced; confidence noted.

## Buildout $/sq ft
- Coffee-shop buildout: $75–$150/sq ft low, $150–$300 typical (restroworks.com, pos.toasttab.com).
- Renovation of existing space: $150–$300/sq ft vs $250–$500 new; existing-shell buildout runs 30–50% less than new (homeguide.com, apxconstructiongroup.com).
- **LOCAL ANCHOR: NWA first-generation tenant buildout $100–$150/sq ft** (modernozarksnwa.com/services/commercial — verified quote on page). HIGH confidence.
- Working range for this project: $100–$250/sq ft, NWA skewing low-mid.

## Change-of-use triggers (R-3 → A-2/B)
- Occupancy change forces entire space to current commercial code: fire alarm, possible sprinklers, ADA, egress, commercial MEP, energy code (khconstructioncorp.com, thebuildingcodeforum.com, neiman-law.com).
- ADA "path of travel" 20% rule: accessible route/restroom upgrades required up to 20% of alteration cost (adata.org). MEDIUM on dollar figures.

## Plumbing
- Rough-in package (toilet, vanity, mop sink, 3-comp, floor sink, hand sink): ~$3,800–$4,500 labor (anecdotal quotes, cakecentral/justanswer). Budget $5K–$15K total with 2nd-floor stack access. MEDIUM.
- Grease trap, under-sink hydromechanical (café-appropriate): $500–$2,000 total basic install (rotorooter.com, caplumbingpointinc.com). In-ground gravity interceptor (unlikely needed coffee-only): $4K–$10K+. HIGH.

## Electrical
- 200A panel upgrade $1,200–$3,000; commercial 200A ~$2,650–$3,000, 400A ~$4,000+ (angi.com, pennaelectric.com). HIGH.
- Café load reality: espresso machine ~20–30A @ 208–240V, dishwasher up to 50A, water heater 30A; a busy café can need 300–400A (thecoffeebrewers.com, majestycoffee.com). Budget $3K–$10K+ if new service/transformer needed. MEDIUM-HIGH.

## HVAC
- Commercial HVAC $15–$34/sq ft installed → $15K–$34K for 1,000 sq ft (nationalfacilitycontractors.com, fastechus.com). If existing residential system recent, code-minimum upgrade (outdoor air per IMC) may be ~$8K–$15K. HIGH/MEDIUM.

## Fire/life-safety
- Fire alarm: $1–$5/sq ft; small business <5,000 sq ft typically $2,000–$5,000; monitoring $30–$100/mo (securityalarm.com, getsafeandsound.com). HIGH.
- Sprinkler retrofit IF required: $2–$7/sq ft typical → ~$2K–$10K for 1,000 sq ft, plus possible new water service/riser (chesapeakesprinkler.com, smokeguard.com). A small 2-story A-2 under ~100 occupants often avoids sprinklers under IBC 903.2.1.2 — verify with Bentonville Fire Marshal. MEDIUM on requirement.

## ADA restroom
- Full ADA single commercial restroom: $15K–$50K+ (revolutionflorida.com); realistic conversion from existing residential bath in NWA: **$10K–$25K** (homeadvisor.com, dadimprovement.com). HIGH.

## Hoods — the biggest avoidable cost
- Type I full system (hood + exhaust + makeup air + Ansul + duct, installed): **$20K–$55K** — the $15–40K rule of thumb is verified and arguably low (vacommercialhvac.com, therestaurantwarehouse.com). Ansul suppression alone $3,000–$5,500; makeup air $3,500–$10,000+.
- Type II (heat/steam only): canopy $1,800–$9,000, far cheaper installed (hoodmart.com, mrhoodusa.com).
- **Ventless strategy (recommended): TurboChef/Merrychef rapid-cook ovens are UL-certified ventless — no hood. $10K–$20K per oven (entry units from ~$4K)** (katom.com, pciauctions.com, webstaurantstore.com). A $12–18K ventless oven avoids a $25–50K Type I system. Menu limited to low-grease items. HIGH.

## Vertical access (the make-or-break item)
- **ADA elevator exemption CONFIRMED — 2010 ADA Standards §206.2.3:** no elevator required if building is under 3 stories OR under 3,000 sq ft per story. Exemption does NOT apply to shopping centers/malls or health-provider offices (corada.com §206.2.3; Title III TA Manual III-5.4000).
- **⚠ CAUTION: a multi-tenant retail building can be deemed a "shopping center" (2+ sales establishments) — bookstore + café could void the exemption.** Arkansas's adopted IBC/ANSI A117.1 applies independently. Confirm with Bentonville Building Inspection. Even when exempt, identical goods/services must be available on an accessible level.
- LULA elevator installed: $60K–$70K standard 2-stop ($50K–$100K+ full range) (cibessymmetry.com, homeelevators.com). HIGH.
- Vertical platform lift (VPL) commercial: ~$10K–$25K+ installed (angi.com, retirementliving.com, deltaaccessibility.com). HIGH.

## Second means of egress
- IBC: 2 exits generally required from A-2 above first floor when occupant load >49; single exit OK for small spaces ≤49 occupants and ≤75 ft travel — verify locally. Keeping occupant load ≤49 is a design lever.
- Exterior steel/aluminum egress stair installed: **$10K–$30K** (triangulated: biggerpockets quote ~$14–15K; homeadvisor $150–$275/step; keuka-studios $15–30K custom; prefab from lapeyrestair.com). Engineering stamp $500–$1,500. MEDIUM.

## Bottom-line scenarios (1,000 sq ft, ventless menu, NWA pricing)
| Scenario | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Lean (ventless, no sprinkler, exemption or VPL, existing bath upgraded) | ~$90K–$150K ($90–$150/sq ft) |
| Mid (Type II hood, sprinkler retrofit, new ADA restroom, egress stair) | ~$150K–$250K |
| Heavy (Type I hood, LULA elevator, 400A service) | $250K–$400K+ |

## Binding constraints (all need the pre-app meeting)
1. Bentonville's adopted IBC edition + fire marshal's sprinkler determination.
2. Whether the building counts as a "shopping center" for the ADA elevator exemption (bookstore downstairs is the question).
3. Occupant-load design (≤49 keeps single-exit and possibly avoids sprinkler/A-2 issues — consider targeting B occupancy with <50 occupants).
