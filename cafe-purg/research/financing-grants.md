# Research: Financing a $170K–$350K First-Time Café in Bentonville (2026)

Confidence: HIGH = primary source; MED = reputable secondary; LOW = confirm directly.

## SBA paths
- **Microloan**: up to $50K (avg ~$13K), 8–13%, ≤7 yr, via mission intermediaries who underwrite character/plan over credit score — the standard answer to thin credit (sba.gov, HIGH). AR intermediaries: **FORGE Community Loan Fund** (Huntsville — NWA's home CDFI, to $50K/6yr; rates unpublished, call), **Communities Unlimited** (Fayetteville CDFI, $1K–$100K), **LiftFund** (Fayetteville office, to ~$50K startup).
- **7(a) small loan** (≤$500K): rates ~Prime+2.75% (≈9–11.5% mid-2026); SOP floor 10% equity injection but **first-time restaurants realistically need 20–30% down** (pursuitlending.com/cdcloans.com, HIGH/MED). Lenders want FICO ~650–680+, 24-mo monthly projections, DSCR ≥1.15x, and ideally industry experience — **naming an experienced café manager in the plan materially changes approval odds**. A documented **family gift counts as equity injection** (gift letter + source verification).
- **Arkansas Capital Corporation (ACC Capital)**: nonprofit statewide SBA lender since 1957, $50K–$5M, explicitly does early-stage deals banks decline — natural anchor lender (acccapital.com, HIGH).
- **Community Advantage**: now CA SBLCs (7(a) to $350K via CDFIs); 2025–26 licensing churn — verify a lender is actively originating (sba.gov + Next City, MED).

## The family-LLC landlord question (verified against SOP commentary)
- The strict EPC/OC related-party rules (13 CFR §120.111 — rent capped at loan payment, etc.) apply **only when SBA money finances the real estate**. Family LLC already owns the building with no SBA debt → those rules don't apply (Starfield & Smith; SOP 50 10 8, HIGH).
- What DOES apply: lease term + borrower-only renewals ≥ loan term (10 yrs); landlord's waiver/lien subordination + collateral assignment of lease — **Craig's LLC must be willing to sign these** (easy with family). Relationship disclosed on SBA forms.
- **Set rent at documented market rate** (broker comp letter): below-market inflates projected cash flow, above-market looks like disguised distributions. Market-rate documentation strengthens the application (MED — lender-counsel commentary; exact SOP cite not pinned).
- **Landlord-funded TI amortized into rent** (standard structure, 7–10% amortization rate): the family LLC capitalizes $25–75K of fixed buildout (hood, walk-in, restroom — things that stay with the building), shrinking the cash project before any loan is sized. **The single most powerful lever in the stack** (Cauble Group / AQUILA, HIGH).

## NWA grant ecosystem — precise version
- **Walton Family Foundation: does NOT fund private businesses directly**; no unsolicited proposals; Design Excellence pays design fees for municipal/nonprofit projects only. WFF money reaches founders through funded intermediaries: **Kiva NWA ($1M match)**, EforAll NWA, Startup Junkie (waltonfamilyfoundation.org, HIGH). Do not budget WFF grants.
- **Kiva NWA Hub** (run by Startup Junkie Foundation): **0% interest, no-fee crowdfunded loans up to $15,000, no credit score requirement** (social underwriting), Walton-matched; active as of Feb 2026 (kiva-nwa.org, HIGH). Excellent first money + doubles as pre-marketing.
- **EforAll NWA**: free year-long accelerator (English/Spanish), open to main-street food businesses; quarterly All Ideas Pitch Contest ($500–$1,000) (eforall.org, HIGH).
- **Startup Junkie**: free 1:1 consulting + capital-access navigation (startupjunkie.org, HIGH). **ASBTDC** (UA): free consulting + loan packaging — lenders like ASBTDC-reviewed projections.
- **Downtown Bentonville Inc.**: no façade/storefront grant currently exists (verify with DBI); value = First Friday/Farmers Market vendor slots for pre-launch revenue + visibility (MED).
- **Dead/wrong-fit**: ARK Challenge (dead 2015), Heartland Challenge (grad students only; paused 2026), Onward FX (scale-stage), Fuel/Tyson (B2B foodtech). NWA pitch money for a café = $500–$5K validation, not capital.

## Equipment financing
- Terms 24–72 mo; realistic startup rates ~8–15% (advertised "from 3.25%" is not for thin credit); Kaldi-type lessors want FICO ~700 for startups (crestmontcapital.com / kaldi.com, MED).
- **Vendor 0% promos exist**: Pro Coffee Gear × Navitas Credit 0% for 12–36 months (procoffeegear.com, MED). Otherwise fold equipment into the 7(a) — often cleaner.
- Finance-to-own ($1 buyout) beats FMV leasing for 10-yr-life espresso equipment.

## Sanity-checked $250K stack
| Layer | Amount | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Founder + family equity/gift | $60K (24%) | Gift letters ready; 20–30% is the real first-timer bar |
| SBA 7(a) (ACC Capital et al.) | $100K, 10 yr, ~10–12% | Needs DSCR ≥1.15x + named experienced operator |
| SBA microloan (FORGE/CU) | $35K, 6 yr, 8–13% | Disclose to 7(a) lender; don't max at $50K |
| Equipment financing | $30K, 36–60 mo | Or fold into 7(a) if credit thin |
| Kiva NWA | $15K, 0% | Credit-blind; community pre-marketing |
| Pre-sold memberships | $10K | Deferred revenue (a liability) — buzz, not equity |
| **Off-stack: landlord TI in rent** | $25–75K | Shrinks everything above |

## Recommended sequence
1. ASBTDC + Startup Junkie (free) → plan + 24-mo projections; enroll EforAll; enter pitch contest.
2. **Lease first**: 10+ yr market-rate lease from family LLC (broker comp letter), landlord waiver pre-agreed, TI amortized at ~7–8%.
3. Kiva NWA $15K campaign (doubles as demand test marketing).
4. SBA 7(a) ~$100–125K via ACC Capital (parallel-quote CU/LiftFund + Arvest/Signature SBA desks), $50–60K documented injection.
5. Microloan gap-fill $25–35K, sized to DSCR.
6. Memberships/gift cards $5–10K at T-60–90 days + DBI vendor slots.

Risks: first-time-operator discount (hire named manager); related-party optics (market-rate documentation); CA program churn (ACC conventional 7(a) as primary path).
