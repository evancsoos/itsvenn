# Research: Demand Evidence — NA Venues + Local 18–21 Population

Collected June 2026. Confidence flags: HIGH = primary source; MED = reputable secondary; LOW = directional.

## A. Comparable concept performance (the honest part)

### Sober bars — high failure/pivot rate even in huge markets
- **Sans Bar (Austin, est. 2017)** — survives, but operates essentially ONE NIGHT A WEEK (Fri 6pm–midnight) + pop-ups; $15 cover w/ unlimited NA drinks; pop-ups draw up to ~300 (founder-reported). Founder's own playbook: pop-ups to build an audience BEFORE signing a lease. (thesansbar.com; communityimpact.com Jan 2025; HIGH on status, MED on attendance.)
- **Hekate (NYC East Village)** — open; survives as café + bottle shop + event venue hybrid. (hekatenyc.com, HIGH.)
- **The Open Road (Pittsburgh)** — open but is a daytime NA bottle shop, not a late-night comp. (openroadbarpgh.com, HIGH.)
- **Listen Bar (NYC)** — never sustained a permanent location; pop-up only since 2018. (listen.bar/about, HIGH.)
- **LA failure cluster**: The New Bar (2 locations) closed; Stay Zero Proof opened Jan 2024, closed ~8 months later. (LAist; Hoodline; LA Magazine — HIGH.)
- **Why they fail** (LAist owner interviews, MED): big-box retail killed bottle sales; sober-curious visitors outnumber sober regulars ("a lot of sober people aren't thinking about sitting in bars"); $14 mocktail price resistance; survivors layered in food/comedy/games/yoga — became ACTIVITY venues.
- No sober bar publishes revenue. (HIGH that the data gap is real.)

### Other late-night alcohol-free models
- **Board game cafés (Snakes & Lattes)** — strongest structural comp: cover charge for unlimited play + F&B + retail; Toronto ~CAD $7M/yr by 2018 across 3 locations; but practitioner warning: long dwell times + sq-ft per customer = "packed house, barely breaking even." Parent company financials are sketchy (penny stock) — treat numbers skeptically. (Wikipedia; BGG economics thread; MED.)
- **Korean-style 24h study cafés** — Korea pricing ~$1.50–2.60/hr or ~$75/mo membership; NO verified US chain wave — genuine evidence gap, not a proven category here. (Stars and Stripes; Korea Times; MED/LOW.)
- **Late-night dessert cafés** — qualitative trend coverage only (UK university towns); no revenue data. (LOW.)

### Membership conversion/churn for young people
- No published benchmark for 18–21 space memberships ANYWHERE (HIGH that gap is real).
- Imperfect proxies: coffee/F&B subscriptions churn 5–10%/mo; 50–70% of subscribers gone by month 3 (eightx.co; MED). Groundfloor SF social-club café: $200–279/mo, ~1,400 members, but ages 30–60 and self-reported (LOW).

### Gen Z drinking/spending
- **Gallup Aug 2025: US drinking at 90-year low (54%); only 50% of 18–34s drink at all; 66% of young adults say moderate drinking is harmful.** (news.gallup.com — HIGH. The single best-documented fact supporting the concept.)
- Counterpoint (MED, trade press): 21–28s spend MORE per visit at REGULAR bars — "sober-ish trading up," not venue-switching. No reliable per-outing spend data exists for under-21s (alcohol-industry trackers start at 21).

## B. Local target population
- **NWACC: record 9,017 credit students Fall 2024 & 2025; 10,706 total incl. workforce (+4.2% YoY).** (nwacc.edu; NWA Democrat-Gazette — HIGH.) Caveat: many part-time/older commuters.
- **NWACC Bentonville campus master plan approved April 2025**: capacity 12,000→30,000 students, ~3,000 on-campus housing units, Walton-funded design, ~$1.5B over 5–20 years. (Talk Business; Axios NWA — HIGH on approval; LOW for near-term demand.)
- **Benton County 18–24: ~26,000–28,000** (8.6% of 321,566; derived — MED). Bentonville city ~4,500–5,000 aged 18–24 (~2,500–3,000 aged 18–21) — LOW extrapolation; pull Census B01001 before putting in a deck.
- **Feeder: ~1,400 new 18-year-olds/year** from Bentonville HS (819 seniors) + Bentonville West (595) per NCES — HIGH. Bella Vista students already counted (no HS of own).
- **U of A Fayetteville: record 34,175 (Fall 2025)** — HIGH on number, LOW that they drive 30 min on weeknights (Dickson St. serves them).
- **"Nothing to do" sentiment: no quantifiable data exists** — no survey, no council report. Only directional: Visit Bentonville nightlife listings are dominated by 21+ bars; one NA-friendly spot (The Hibiscus, spirit-free menu, ~11pm Fri–Sat). (HIGH that the data doesn't exist.)

## Sober assessment (what goes in the business case)
1. Macro tailwind is real and strong (HIGH): half of 18–34s don't drink; gap + ~1,400 new local 18-year-olds/yr.
2. NWACC growth + planned on-campus housing is the most favorable local fact.
3. **The comp set is a warning: "NA bar" is a failing format. The surviving shape is "late-night ACTIVITY venue that happens to be alcohol-free"** — games, study hours, desserts, events, cover-charge nights. Café Purg should be positioned this way explicitly.
4. Risk to respect: 18–21s are the lowest-income segment + long dwell times = full room at break-even unless per-head spend is engineered (covers, memberships, desserts, time-based pricing).
5. **Only honest demand test = pre-sale**: founding memberships with refund trigger + Sans Bar-style $15-cover pop-up nights BEFORE buildout. This is now elevated from "good idea" to "the load-bearing step."
