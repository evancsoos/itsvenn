# Research: Bentonville Change-of-Use, Zoning, Fire/ADA (verified June 2026)

**Critical context: Bentonville replaced its entire development code. The new Bentonville Community Code (Chapter 50) was adopted April 14, 2026, effective May 14, 2026.** Anything published before May 2026 (including the old "DC Downtown Core" district) is superseded. Zoning findings below are from the adopted Chapter 50 text (bentonville.municipalcodeonline.com; bentonville.ar.gov/882/Zoning-Code).

## Change of use / occupancy — process
- No standalone "change of use" permit; a previously occupied space requires a **Commercial Alteration/Remodel Permit** via the **eTrakit** portal (Commercial Submittals Checklist, updated Mar 2026: bentonville.ar.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4237; bentonville.ar.gov/181/Commercial-Applications).
- Required at submittal: stamped construction drawings (architect stamp required >$250K; practically needed for change of occupancy regardless) incl. occupancy load, travel distances, MEP plans; **ADH approval required PRIOR to city permit approval** for food service; shell footprint drawing; **Business Registry certificate approved before permit issues**.
- Separate **Kitchen Hood & Duct Suppression permit** if installing a hood.
- Review by **Building & Fire Safety Division** (inside Bentonville Fire Dept — building official + fire marshal unified). **Todd Pearce, Division Chief/Fire Marshal, todd.pearce@bentonville.ar.gov, 479-271-3108**; division 479-271-3151, 305 SW A Street.
- Code basis: **2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code** (eff. 1/1/2023) = amended 2021 IFC (Vol I) + 2021 IBC (Vol II). R→A-2/B is a *more* hazardous change, so expect full new-occupancy life-safety compliance (IEBC alternative path possible with building official approval).

## Zoning — the square under the new code
- Square area is almost certainly **T5.2 Town Center High** ("reflects the look and feel of downtown Bentonville"; 6 stories/96 ft; fact sheet: bentonville.ar.gov/DocumentCenter/View/20634). ⚠ Confirm parcel via city GIS or Planning 479-271-3122.
- **"Food and beverage" is allowed BY RIGHT (■) in T5.1, T5.2, T6, GC, LI, LFI** — Sec. 50-406 Table 4.2 District Use Matrix, pulled from adopted code text. No conditional use permit. The 1,600 sq ft tenant cap applies only in T2.1/T2.2/T4.2, not T5.2. Upper-story location not restricted.
- **Parking: NO minimum car parking for food & beverage in T5/T6** (Table 5.9). Bicycle: 0.5 short-term spaces/1,000 sq ft.
- **Downtown Center Overlay** (Ord. 2026-78, Sec. 50-405/50-506): objective standards on materials/heights/shopfronts — matters mainly if altering the façade or adding signage/awnings. Bentonville chose NOT to create a historic design review board (bentonvillebulletin.com).

## Pre-application meetings — free, the right first move
- **Conceptual Meeting** (first step): email planning@bentonville.ar.gov or call **479-271-3122**.
- **Pre-Application Conference**: Wednesdays at the Municipal Complex; request by the preceding Wednesday; checklist requires site plan, project description, floor plan with Preliminary Architectural Code Analysis (bentonville.ar.gov/DocumentCenter/View/19889). No fee listed for either.
- For an interior-only change of use, a conceptual meeting + direct conversation with Building & Fire Safety (479-271-3108) may suffice — confirm with staff.

## Occupancy classification — THE design lever
- Food/drink consumption = **Group A-2**, BUT **IBC 303.1.1: assembly space with occupant load <50 = Group B** (up.codes/viewer/arkansas/ibc-2021).
- Occupant load: dining 15 sq ft/person net (Table 1004.5); a dining area under ~700–750 net sq ft stays below 50. A 900–1,200 sq ft loft pencils to ~30–60 occupants depending on layout — **design deliberately around the 29/49 thresholds.**

## ADA / second-floor access (highest-risk item)
- **2010 ADA Standards 206.2.3 Exception 1**: private facilities <3 stories OR <3,000 sq ft/story exempt from accessible route between stories — unless shopping center/mall, health-care office, or transit. A café is not on the exclusion list, and **DOJ's "shopping center" definition for this purpose covers buildings housing FIVE or more sales/rental establishments** — bookstore + café would not normally meet it. (ada.gov 2010 Standards; corada.com 206-2-3.) [Note: an earlier research pass suggested 2+ tenants could trigger it; the verified DOJ definition is 5+.]
- Exemption excuses only the vertical route — everything inside the café (restroom, counter, clearances) must comply; path-of-travel obligations up to 20% of alteration cost apply.
- **IBC side: 1104.4** parallel exception (no accessible route to stories ≤3,000 sq ft aggregate, with carve-outs). IBC 1108.2.9 (accessible dining) presumes same services available in an accessible space — for a café existing ONLY on an inaccessible second floor, expect discussion with the building official. **Bottom line: law plausibly does not require an elevator, but get it confirmed in writing by the building official and an AR-licensed architect before committing.** Mitigations: ground-floor service/pickup point shared with the bookstore.

## Fire / life safety
- **Sprinklers: IBC 903.2.1.2 requires sprinklers for Group A-2 located on a floor other than level of exit discharge — a second-floor A-2 automatically triggers them.** Group B (<50 occupants) has no equivalent trigger. Strongest argument for staying under 50.
- **Egress (Table 1006.3.4(2))**: second story single exit allowed only for B/F/M/S with **≤29 occupants and ≤75 ft travel**; Group A never single-exit from second story. So: ≤29 occupants = one compliant stair may suffice; 30–49 = two means of egress; ≥50 = A-2 + sprinklers + panic hardware. **Survey the building's stairs before committing.**
- Also expect: fire-rated separation from bookstore, emergency lighting/exit signage, possible fire alarm; hood suppression permit if cooking.

## Other local items
- **Business Registry: FREE**, annual renewal, via eTrakit, required before building permit. 479-271-3126, businessregistry@bentonvillear.com.
- **Sign permit** required (Sec. 50-508/50-721(h)); projecting signs need encroachment permit. Fee unpublished — ⚠ confirm.
- **A&P (HMR) tax: 1% on prepared food and non-alcoholic beverages**, remitted monthly to Bentonville A&P Commission (visitbentonville.com/partners/hmr-tax). Combined sales tax ≈9.5% — ⚠ verify with DFA.

## Fees & timeline
- Building permit (valuation-based): $150K buildout ≈ **$730**; plus trade sub-permits, hood permit if any, sign permit, ADH plan review (≤$500), $35 ADH permit (bentonville.ar.gov/156).
- No published city review-time commitment; Permit Place (unofficial) estimates commercial review **4–12 weeks, usually one cycle**; small tenant alterations at the short end.
- **Realistic end-to-end: pre-app (1–2 wks) → design + ADH plan review (4–8 wks) → city review (3–8 wks) → construction → inspections + CO. Plan 4–7 months from architect engagement to opening.**

## Risk flags
1. Parcel zoning unconfirmed (T5.2 is a characterization — confirm with address).
2. Vertical accessibility — exemption very likely applies, but needs written sign-off. Highest risk.
3. Second egress — single-stair caps at 29 occupants; larger needs second path.
4. Sprinklers — automatic if A-2 (≥50) on second floor; avoidable under 50.
5. **Structural: assembly live load is 100 psf vs 40 psf residential — historic floor capacity must be checked by a structural engineer.** Unverified locally.
